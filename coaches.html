PAGEANT COACH & MENTOR

Chinna Mack

Specialties: Interview Preparation • Stage Presence • Modeling & Walking • Confidence Building • Personal Development • Pageant Attire Consultant

I have been in pageantry for over ten years. I have competed and held many titles with many different systems such as Pure International, American Inspirational Miss, AmeriFest, and Miss Spectacular America.

I have mentored my daughter Jayla as well as many other contestants. I have walked for New York Fashion Week multiple times.

I have extensive knowledge of how to prepare for all types of pageants, including glam, high glitz, and non-glitz. My experience includes Interview Preparation, Stage Presence, Modeling & Walking, Confidence Building, Personal Development, and Pageant Attire Consulting.

I look forward to working with and helping all my contestants throughout their pageant journey!

PAGEANT COACH & MENTOR

Julianna Langer

Specialties: Interview Preparation • Stage Presence • Modeling & Walking • Confidence Building • Personal Development • Pageant Attire Consulting

Julianna brings more than ten years of pageant experience as both a contestant and mentor. Throughout her pageant journey, she has competed in and held numerous titles with systems including Pure International, American Inspirational Miss, and Miss Spectacular America.

Her experience extends beyond the pageant stage. Julianna has mentored and helped coach contestants. She also serves as Miss Beautiful & Elegance USA's Podcast Director and Creator. Through The Crown & Conversation, hosted by Julie, she discusses everything contestants need to know about pageantry. She mentors her audience by covering hot topics, fashion, attire, and the true ins and outs of the pageant world. She provides valuable information for everyone from beginners to experienced pageant queens.

With extensive knowledge of pageantry, Julianna understands that every contestant and every pageant system requires a unique approach. She specializes in interview preparation, stage presence, modeling and walking, confidence building, personal development, and pageant attire consulting.

Like Chinna, Julianna is passionate about helping contestants feel prepared, confident, and ready to shine. She looks forward to supporting each contestant as they grow, achieve their goals, and enjoy their own unique pageant journey.
