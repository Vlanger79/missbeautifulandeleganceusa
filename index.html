THE CROWN AWAITS

Your Story Deserves a Stage.

From first-time contestants to queens ready to represent their state or the nation, there is a place for your dream in the Miss Beautiful & Elegance USA family.

Meet Julie — Our First Legacy Queen 👑

Julie has been part of the pageant world since she was five years old and carries a lifelong love for pageants and the friends who become family. She serves her community with heart and brings experience in judging, interviewing, and coaching. She is our Legacy!

A Legacy title is a lifetime honor earned by living out the values that define a Legacy Queen: Beauty, Confidence, Purpose, Elegance, a Heart for Giving Back, Dedication, Determination, Poise, and Love for Family, Friends, and Community.

This stage is for girls and women of all ages. If you are driven to be bigger than yourself and have the determination to begin a new journey, then you belong with Miss Beautiful & Elegance USA.

You will have a team of skilled and caring mentors cheering you on every step of the way—and maybe you could be our next Legacy Queen.

PAGEANT COACH & MENTOR

Chinna Mack

Specialties: Interview Preparation • Stage Presence • Modeling & Walking • Confidence Building • Personal Development • Pageant Attire Consultant

I have been in pageantry for over ten years. I have competed and held many titles with many different systems such as Pure International, American Inspirational Miss, AmeriFest, and Miss Spectacular America.

I have mentored my daughter Jayla as well as many other contestants. I have walked for New York Fashion Week multiple times.

I have extensive knowledge of how to prepare for all types of pageants, including glam, high glitz, and non-glitz. My experience includes Interview Preparation, Stage Presence, Modeling & Walking, Confidence Building, Personal Development, and Pageant Attire Consulting.

I look forward to working with and helping all my contestants throughout their pageant journey!

PAGEANT COACH & MENTOR

Julianna Langer

Specialties: Interview Preparation • Stage Presence • Modeling & Walking • Confidence Building • Personal Development • Pageant Preparation

Julianna brings more than ten years of pageant experience as both a contestant and mentor. Throughout her pageant journey, she has competed in and held titles with Pure International, American Inspirational Miss, and Miss Spectacular America.

Her experience extends beyond the pageant stage. Julianna has mentored and helped coach contestants. She also serves as Miss Beautiful & Elegance USA's Podcast Director "The Crown & Conversation".

With extensive knowledge of pageantry, Julianna understands that every contestant and every pageant system requires a unique approach. She specializes in helping contestants prepare with confidence, poise, and purpose.

Like Chinna, Julianna is passionate about helping contestants feel prepared, confident, and ready to shine. She looks forward to supporting each contestant throughout their pageant journey.
